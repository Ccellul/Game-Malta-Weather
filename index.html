Hunting & Trapping Rules

🐇 Rabbit Hunting

  • Season: 1st June to 31st December.
  • Permission from landowner is mandatory.
  • Max 2 dogs per hunter.

🦆 Bird Hunting (Land)

  • Autumn: 1st Sept to 31st Jan.
  • Spring: Specific dates announced yearly (usually April).
  • Must wear carnation/orange hat/vest if required by law.

🕸️ Finches (7 Species) Trapping

  • Research period: As per derogation (20th Oct to 20th Dec).
  • Live decoys must be ringed.
  • All birds caught must be released after recording.

🏺 Plover & Song Thrush

  • Season: 20th Oct to 10th Jan.
  • Manual or whistle decoys only.
  • Specific mesh sizes apply to nets.
